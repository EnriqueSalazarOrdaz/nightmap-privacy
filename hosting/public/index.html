NightMap Privacy Policy

Android, iPhone, and iPad apps · com.nightmap.app · com.nightmap.ios

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the NightMap mobile application ("NightMap", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with your use of the Android, iPhone, or iPad app. The Android app uses the package identifier com.nightmap.app; the iPhone and iPad app uses the bundle identifier com.nightmap.ios. By installing or using NightMap, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

NightMap is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or contact details to use the core features of the app.

However, some data may be collected or processed when you use the app:

  • Device and diagnostic data: On Android, Google Analytics for Firebase may process a pseudonymous app-instance identifier, advertising identifier, approximate location inferred from network information, device and app details, sessions, screen views, app interactions, and purchase or subscription events. Firebase Crashlytics may process crash stack traces, relevant app state, app and operating-system versions, device model, technical metadata, timestamps, installation/session identifiers, and application-not-responding reports. Crash reports may include Analytics breadcrumb events. NightMap does not intentionally send names, email addresses, manual map choices, map coordinates, purchase tokens, or user-entered text in these reports. On iPhone and iPad, NightMap writes the StoreKit restore error domain and code to the device’s system log and displays those values in the app; it does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.
  • Advertising identifiers: When ads are shown, Google AdMob and its partners may access and process advertising identifiers and related device information (see Section 4). Availability and use depend on the platform, device settings, and consent choices.
  • Credits and usage: Your credit balance and map selections are stored or processed locally on your device. On iPhone and iPad, rewarded credits are also granted locally after a completed rewarded ad. On Android, Google Analytics for Firebase receives rewarded-ad events, including technical ad status and the reward type/amount; the app does not send your credit balance or manual map choices to Analytics.
  • Purchase and subscription status: Whether you hold an active Pro subscription is checked through the store for the platform you use (Google Play Billing on Android or Apple App Store and StoreKit on iPhone and iPad; see Section 5).

We do not sell your personal information.

iPhone and iPad: Credits are stored locally on your device. Map choices and matching are processed locally. During an App Store restore failure, NightMap records and displays the error domain and code through the device’s system logging facility; the iOS app does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. AdMob and UMP communicate with Google to provide ads and manage consent, and StoreKit communicates with Apple to handle purchases and subscription status.

Android: Credits and app state are stored on your device, while Google Analytics for Firebase records app usage and ad events, and Firebase Crashlytics reports release crashes and application-not-responding events. Google Play Billing handles purchases and subscription status. AdMob and UMP process information needed for advertising and consent. See the platform-specific sections below for more detail. These providers process information under their own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the app and its features.
  • To display advertising to free users and to deliver rewarded ads.
  • To grant and track credits, including credits earned by watching rewarded ads.
  • To manage and verify Pro subscription status.
  • To diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse, and improve the app.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Free Users, Credits, and Pro Subscription

NightMap offers a free tier and a paid Pro subscription.

  • Free users receive credits and may see banner ads. Free users can earn additional credits by voluntarily watching rewarded video ads. Each completed rewarded ad grants 1 credit.
  • Pro users (product ID nightmap_pro_monthly) receive unlimited searches and an ad-free experience.

Your credit balance and subscription status may be stored on your device and/or processed by the services described below in order to deliver these features.

4. Ads and Rewarded Ads

NightMap uses Google AdMob on Android and iPhone/iPad to display banner ads to free users and to deliver rewarded video ads that grant credits upon completion. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process data, including advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and ad interaction data, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent choices and applicable regional requirements.

On Android, Google Analytics for Firebase also records app and monetization activity, including rewarded-ad request, load, show, failure, and reward events with technical event details. On iPhone and iPad, the app does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics; AdMob and UMP may still send their own service, advertising, and consent-related data to Google.

Consent (Google UMP)

Where required by applicable law (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other applicable regions), NightMap uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to present a consent flow before requesting ads. The app checks UMP’s consent state before initializing AdMob and requesting ads. UMP stores or returns the consent signal needed by the advertising SDK; your choices determine whether ads can be requested and whether personalized advertising is available. When UMP requires it, you can reopen Google’s privacy options from the app.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use apps that integrate AdMob, see Google’s Privacy & Terms .

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Android: Pro subscriptions are sold and processed through Google Play Billing. Payment information you provide is handled directly by Google and the Google Play Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Android subscriptions are managed or cancelled in the Google Play Store under your Google account’s subscriptions.

iPhone and iPad: Pro subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store and handled in the app using StoreKit. Apple handles payment information; we do not receive or store your full payment card details. iOS subscriptions are managed or cancelled in your Apple Account’s subscription settings (for example, Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or through the App Store. Apple’s handling of your data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy .

On iOS, NightMap uses StoreKit 2 on the device to look up the product, purchase it, restore purchases, and check verified subscription entitlements. The app has no server-side purchase verification service.

For Android purchases, Google Play’s handling of your data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy .

6. Cookies, Identifiers, and Advertising ID

NightMap does not use traditional browser cookies. However, the app and its third-party services may use device identifiers and similar technologies to deliver and measure advertising and to support core functionality. On Android, this may include the Google Advertising ID. On iPhone and iPad, the app includes SKAdNetwork identifiers for ad attribution. Google’s advertising SDK may process platform-provided advertising and device identifiers as permitted by device settings and consent choices.

On most Android devices you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization through your device settings, typically under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path may vary by device).

7. Third-Party Services

NightMap relies on third-party services that may independently collect and process data according to their own privacy policies. These include, but are not limited to:

  • Google AdMob — AdMob & AdSense data
  • Google Play / Google Play Billing — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent services
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for iPhone and iPad purchases and subscription status

On Android only, the app also uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. See Firebase privacy and security information .

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

8. Children’s Privacy

NightMap is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the app’s features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Google Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, UMP, Google Play, and Apple retain data they process according to their respective settings and policies. Locally stored data, such as your credit balance, can generally be cleared by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

10. Your Choices and Consent

  • Ad consent: Where required, you can accept or decline personalized advertising through the consent flow presented in the app.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.
  • Subscriptions: Android subscriptions can be managed or cancelled through Google Play; iPhone and iPad subscriptions can be managed or cancelled through your Apple Account subscription settings or the App Store.
  • App data: You can remove locally stored data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling NightMap.
  • Google privacy choices: On iOS, reopen the privacy options in NightMap when the option is required; on Android, use the in-app privacy controls where available and your device’s ad settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights regarding your personal data, such as the right to access, correct, or delete it. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
